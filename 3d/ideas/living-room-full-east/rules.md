# Idea: living room across the full width at the east end

Living + kitchen + dining is one room spanning the full inner width at the
uphill (east) end. Its view windows sit on its short south side, about 5,5 m.

## Rules

- **Footprint:** a plain rectangle, roughly 9.5–11 m wide and 18–23 m long.
- **Placement** (variant 1, see `terrain/placed-full-east.html`): east wall
  40,9 m up the slope lines, as far east as the 2,8 m shed allows with 2 m to
  the east boundary. Terrace 5,4 × 3 m exactly along the living room portals,
  34,8–40,2 m. Living level 273,5, 0,4 m under the ground at the east wall;
  garage floor 270,7, 2,8 m below. The garden in front of the terrace is raised
  to its level with about 11 m³ of soil dug out on site. The driveway is dug
  down up to 1,1 m over its last ~9 m; the front door sill is 1,1 m above the
  ground in front of it.
- **Living + kitchen + dining** spans the full inner width at the **east** end,
  with the big glazing and a door onto the terrace in its south wall.
- **Roof windows:** two of about 1,14 × 1,40 m over the kitchen counter, in the
  north slope.
- **Big kids room** spans the **full inner width** at the **west** end, with its
  big window on the west wall facing the road.
- **Front door** on the **south** side, towards the front (west) half.
- **Terrace** on the south side by the living room, stopping short of the east
  wall.

## What the earlier variants settled

- The room list lands at **170–175 m² net**, not the 160 that was the target,
  and circulation runs 13–14 % of it. The house comes out 20–22 m long.
- In a house about 10 m wide, the living + kitchen stays at 50–55 m² **only if
  15–25 m² of other rooms sit behind it** on the north side — pantry, laundry,
  storage. Otherwise it grows to 65–75 m² and the house with it.
- The living room's own south wall is only 5,5–6,5 m, so the 8–10 m of south
  glazing comes only as a sum across the whole south facade, split between the
  living room, the bedroom and the kids room.
- The guest room / office ends up with north light in every variant so far. The
  south side is taken by the kids room, the entrance, the bedroom and the living
  room. A variant that gets it south light without stretching the house would be
  worth seeing.
- Ideas already tried, do not repeat them:
  1. corridor spine, wet rooms over the utility room;
  2. wide house 11 m, entrance hall as the hub, bedroom beside the kids room;
  3. narrow house 9,5 m, corridor spine, laundry downstairs — the cheapest plan;
  4. long house 22 m, every habitable room facing south or west;
  5. bedroom between the entrance and a walk-in closet, en-suite at the west
     end, pantry as a strip along the north wall — kept as `variant-1.html`.
