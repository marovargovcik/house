Living room ceiling: one slope or a full A

The whole house has an A roof with the ridge running west–east down the middle. The living room has no flat ceiling, so you see the roof from inside. Its depth (south to north) decides whether you see one slope or both.

Rough: 2,7 m clear height under the flat ceilings, no knee wall, 0,3 m for the flat ceiling and its insulation, 0,44 m outside walls. Heights are to the finished inside of the roof.
