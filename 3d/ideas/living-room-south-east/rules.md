# Idea: living room along the south wall at the east end

Living + kitchen + dining sits in the south-east corner: its long side runs
along the south wall for the view, its short side on the east wall for the
morning sun. Other rooms fill the north-east corner behind it.

## Rules

- **Footprint:** a plain rectangle. The plot sets its length at about 19–21 m
  (below); the width is whatever the room list needs. Report both.
- **Placement:** long axis along the slope lines. Garage door wall 18,9 m up
  the lines, east wall 39,05 m, 2,5 m from the north boundary; the living room
  is centred on the south strip's centre line. Living level 273,40, 0,1–0,4 m
  under the ground at the east wall; garage floor 270,70, 2,7 m below, with
  garage and utility 9 m deep. The ground at the garage door is 0–0,6 m above
  its floor. Shed 7 × 2,8 m against the east wall from the north corner,
  3,8 m from the east boundary. Terrace 7,5 × 3 m along the living room
  portals; the ground in front of it is up to 0,7 m below the floor, raised
  with soil dug out on site. About 340 m³ dug out, footings included.
- **Living + kitchen + dining** in the south-east corner, **52–60 m²**:
  5,5–6,5 m deep (north–south) and 7–9 m long (west–east).
- **Its windows:** 6–8 m of glazing and the terrace door in the south wall,
  always topped up by a window in the east wall.
- **Open ceiling** over the living + kitchen only, up to the underside of the
  roof. Everything else has a flat ceiling with the cold attic over it. The
  attic must stay walkable along the ridge over the flat-ceiling part, with
  room for storage on both sides.
- **Full A:** the living room is deeper than half the house, so the ridge runs
  inside it and the ceiling shows both slopes. The wall between it and the
  north rooms stands on the north slope and goes up to the roof.
- The deeper the room, the shorter its south wall. Each variant states
  the room's depth, how far north of the ridge its north wall stands, and the
  length of south glazing that fits. `ceiling.html` shows the trade.
- **East wall:** the shed covers about 7 m of it at the north end. The south
  part, about 3 m, belongs to the living room and gets windows for the morning
  sun.
- **Big kids room** at the **west** end, big window on the west wall,
  splittable later into two rooms.
- **Front door** on the south side, just east of the kids room.
- **Práčovňa** on this level, about 6–7 m²: washer, dryer, a big freezer and a
  drying rack; small window on the north.
- It sits a short walk from the master bedroom and the kids room, and the walk
  does not cross the living room.
- **Rooms:** kids room, second kids room / guest / office (may be small),
  master bedroom with a walk-in closet if it fits and an en-suite (shower, WC,
  basin), bathroom (tub and shower, no WC), WC with a basin, laundry, pantry,
  entrance hall. Sizes as in `general-rules.md`, leaning to the upper end,
  except the guest room.
- **165–170 m² net** in total.

## What it overrides in `general-rules.md`

- **Living + kitchen + dining** is 52–60 m² instead of 50–55.
- **South glazing:** 6–8 m in the living room instead of roughly 6–10.
- **165–170 m² net** instead of 160–170.
- **Kitchen daylight:** roof windows in the north slope only where that slope
  inside the room is wide enough for them; otherwise from the east wall.
