# Idea: slim and long, garage in the steep step

A narrow house that starts early enough for the garage to sit in the steep step,
with the living room across the full width at the east end, all of it in the
open south view.

## Rules

- **Footprint:** 8 × 26 m. Front wall 14 m up the slope lines, back wall at
  40 m.
- **Garage + utility room** under the western 10,5 m, floor about 270,1 at the
  ground by the door. Living level about 273,1. The stairs up sit inside that
  10,5 m and arrive in the entrance hall.
- **Living + kitchen + dining** across the full width at the east end. Its
  south glazing is centred on about 36 m and all of it lies east of 26,7 m, so
  none of it looks at the neighbour's fence.
- **Big kids room** across the full width at the **west** end, windows facing
  the road, splittable later into a north and a south room.
- **Entrance hall** across the full width east of the kids room: front door on
  the south, stairs from the garage.
- **Corridor** from the hall east to the living room: wet rooms, laundry and
  pantry on its north side; en-suite, master bedroom and guest room on its south
  side, in that order from the west.
- **Terrace** along the living room, centred on about 36 m.
- **East wall:** the shed covers only its northern part, so the living room
  gets an east window south of it.

## Why here

Garage floor at the ground at the door, living level 3 m above. A garage
starting at 13–15 m takes the steep step (17,5–25 m) under its back half, and
the living level sits within ±0,7 m of the ground along its whole length:
almost no fill, short foundation walls, the terrace at garden level. Starting at
20 m lifts the living level up to 1,5 m above the ground. Earth dug out is about
the same either way.

## What it overrides in `general-rules.md`

- **Width 8 m** instead of the default 10; garage depth 10,5 m.
- **East window** in the living room, south of the shed.
